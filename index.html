Waa'ee Koo (About Me)

Maqaan koo Firaol Fayisa Kabeta jedhama. Biyya Itiyoophiyaa Naannoo Oromiyaa, Godina Lixa Oromiyaa Aanaa Boneyya Bosheetti Abbaa koo Fayyisaa Qaabataa Lataa fi Haadha koo Soogiddee Lammeessaa Waaqjiraa irraa bara 1998 G.C. ttin dhaladhe.

Barumsa koo sadarkaa 1ffaa fi 2ffaa Mana Barumsaa Soodduu Mikaa'el fi Billo Secondary & Preparatory School 'tti baradhe. Sana booda Kolleejjii Barnoota Barsiisota Shaambuutti sadarkaa Dippiloomaatiin saayinsii uumamaa (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) barachuun qabxii olaanaa CGPA 3.55'n eebbifame.

Eebbifameen booda Giddu-gala Oromiyaatti qacarameen Mana Barumsaa Sadarkaa 1ffaa irratti waggaa 4f Physics fi Chemistry barsiiseera. Osoo barsiisuu digirii koo jalqabaa Yuunivarsiitii Amboo irraa gosa barnootaa baay'ee jaalladhu Information Technology (IT)n baradhee qabxii olaanaa CGPA 3.72 (Great Distinction)'n eebbifame. Sana boodas Mana Barumsaa Sadarkaa 2ffaa irratti gosa barnootaa Information Technology Waggaa 2f barsiiseera.

Dandeettii dhuunfaa kiyya cimsuuf leenjiiwwan IT dabalataa hedduu (Coding, Computer Security, Maintenance, Hardware & Networking...) fudhadheera. Waajjira Barnootaatti dorgomee Ogeessa ICT fi Digital Park ta'uun hojjedheera.

Amma immoo dandeettii dhuunfaa koo cimsadhee programming languages, coding, fi website adda addaa saxaxuun (designing) namoota barbaadaniif gumaachuun jireenya Digital Ethiopia keessatti qooda fudhachaan jira.

🚀 Walumaagalatti: Pirojektoota adda addaa hojjechuu, Koodingii, Database, Web Development fi barnoota IT wajjin walqabatu kamiirrattiyyuu deeggarsa yoo barbaaddan toora quunnamtii kiyyaan na quunnamaa. Waan dubbistaniif galatoomaa!
